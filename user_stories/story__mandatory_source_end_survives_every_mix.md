# A forbidden recording tail stays excluded from every mix

As a listener, when I identify an abrupt splice or unwanted ending in one
recording, I want one mandatory source cutoff in its global DJ notes so every
future plan and AI harness excludes that audio without needing another warning.

Concrete accepted example: 50 Cent, WHO SHOT YA, 24 Shots (2003), track 06.
The beat changes by 1:33. Never use source audio from 1:32 onward. Complete
the fade before 92 seconds, even under another instrumental or at a new tempo.

Acceptance:

- A conflicting plan note, trusted ride, or full-track request cannot extend
  the latest global cutoff; unrelated recordings remain unaffected.
- Source loads physically exclude the tail before Mixxx can play it.
- A missing encoder or incompatible cue fails before playback.
- Old rendered mixes that contain the tail are rejected and must be rendered
  again; compliant renders retain verifiable source bounds and output hashes.
- Original recordings are preserved. Generated audio lives outside the repo.
- A shorter handoff preserves backbeat matching and finishes before the limit.
