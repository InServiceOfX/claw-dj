# Advanced techniques in Build mix plan

As a DJ, I want Build mix plan to use proven skips, re-entries, stretched short
intros, measured sample blends and instrumental support when the recordings and
my notes justify them, so one build can produce a musical arrangement without
giving up gentle fades, backbeat matching or source protections.

Source: Ernest's 2026-10-06 request to implement the previously recommended
stages, preserved in docs/intents/intent__evidence-gated-advanced-techniques-in-the-generi-7ab3a6e9.md.

Acceptance:

- Advance through same-song handoffs and intro extension, measured sample/source
  unison, then simultaneous instrumental support with owned decks and monitoring.
- Require reliable recording-specific evidence and explicit approvals. Missing,
  stale or conflicting evidence retains the ordinary plan and explains why.
- Preserve finalized songs, order constraints, effective notes, source exclusions,
  verse/phrase timing and the gentle different-song fader rule.
- Keep one supporting layer at a time, matched by backbeat and pitch throughout
  its approved body; stop and restore every owned deck on failure or interruption.
- Preserve hand-authored performances. Build never starts or applies playback.
- Commit and tag the stages; keep secrets and personal output outside Git.
