# claw-dj public demo short

**Date:** 2026-10-19. **Owner:** Ernest. **Tooling:** `scripts/demo/demo_music.py` and `scripts/demo/demo_kit.py`, both driven by `scripts/demo/storyboard.json`.
Rendered media goes to `~/.openclaw/workspace/Data/Public/Videos/claw-dj-demo-short/` and the demo music to `~/.openclaw/workspace/Data/Public/Music/claw-dj-demo/`. Neither is in Git.

The cut is made to be **posted publicly**: YouTube, Shorts, Reels, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, or a reply to anyone (H Company, founders, investors, music executives). It asks for nothing; the call to action is the open-source repo. This doc replaces the 2026-10-18 investor draft, which used commercial music and pitched for meetings.

## The one message

> Ask your AI agent, or click once in the app, and claw-dj builds a real DJ mix from your own library. Then it **performs** it live in Mixxx and **keeps your DJ notes** for refinement.

Most of the screen time is the **GUI** and an **AI coding agent** (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes) driving it.

## What Ernest does: three steps, about 20 minutes

Run everything from the claw-dj folder. **Don't do steps 2–3 while streaming**: they use Mixxx and a lot of CPU.

**0. Watch the draft** (it has a placeholder voice):
`open ~/.openclaw/workspace/Data/Public/Videos/claw-dj-demo-short/`, then open the newest `claw-dj_demo_*_draft_*.mp4`.
To hear the demo music on its own: `afplay ~/.openclaw/workspace/Data/Public/Music/claw-dj-demo/"claw-dj demo - Night Drive.flac"`.

**1. Record your voice on your iPhone** (about 5 minutes). The Mac mini has no built-in mic, and the iPhone sounds better than most USB mics.
1. Optional: in iPhone **Settings › Apps › Voice Memos**, set **Audio Quality** to **Lossless**.
2. Pick a quiet room; a closet full of clothes is ideal. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, a little to the side.
3. Open **Voice Memos**, tap record, and read the nine lines from the table above **in order**, with about **two seconds of silence between lines**. If you fluff a line, just stop and start a new memo; the whole read is under a minute.
4. Stop, then **AirDrop** the memo to the Mac. It lands in `~/Downloads` as an `.m4a`.
5. Split it into the nine lines:
   ```sh
   uv run python scripts/demo/demo_kit.py import-vo ~/Downloads/<the memo>.m4a
   ```
   It cuts at the eight longest pauses and prints each line's length. If it says it couldn't find enough clear pauses, re-record with longer pauses.
- **Alternative at the Mac:** `uv run python scripts/demo/demo_kit.py record-vo` is a line-by-line teleprompter. It needs a mic connected to the Mac, for example the iPhone as a Continuity microphone, or AirPods. Use `--device N` to choose it.
- **One memo per line also works:** AirDrop all nine, then `import-vo ~/Downloads/a.m4a ~/Downloads/b.m4a …` in script order.

**2. Record Mixxx in OBS** (about 2 minutes, after you finish streaming). This makes the payoff shot real.
1. Use an OBS scene that shows the Mixxx window full-frame and captures desktop audio. Click **Start Recording**.
2. Run the demo-music blend:
   ```sh
   uv run python -m hands.audition_transition --plan ~/.openclaw/workspace/Data/Public/Music/claw-dj-demo/mix_plan.json --transition 1
   ```
   - The first load analyzes both new files in Mixxx (BPM and grid), which takes a few seconds.
   - It plays a 32-beat lead-in, the 32-beat blend and a 16-bar tail, then stops itself.
3. Click **Stop Recording**. OBS saves the file to `~/Movies`.

This music is ours, so the recording's audio is safe to post publicly.

**3. Assemble** (about 3 minutes):
```sh
uv run python scripts/demo/demo_kit.py assemble --vo ernest --obs ~/Movies/<the new recording>.mkv
```
- It finds the blend in your recording by matching the audio. It prints `[obs] sync … (match …)`; a weak match means the wrong file.
- Leave off `--obs` to keep the drawn deck shot. It then reads BLEND PREVIEW, not LIVE IN MIXXX.

Outputs: `claw-dj_demo_wide_ernest_<time>.mp4` and `claw-dj_demo_vertical_ernest_<time>.mp4`. Post them yourself.

**Fast checks without encoding:** `assemble --frames` writes two PNG frames per shot to `preview/`.

## Re-taking the GUI stills (only if the GUI changes)

## Music

- `demo_music.py` synthesizes everything with numpy: drums, bass, Rhodes FM, pad, pluck and reverb. There are no samples and no third-party loops.
- Seed 7, so every run gives the same two files.
- Each track starts with a kick on beat 1 at 0.000 s, so its grid is exact.
- Its spectral balance was checked against Sade "Paradise" and Maxwell "Ascension".
- If your ear says it's too plain, the cheapest upgrade is better demo music, not more editing:
  - change `NIGHT_DRIVE` / `VELVET_HOUR` in `demo_music.py`;
  - or drop in two tracks you own the rights to, and point `blend.plan` at a plan that plays them.
